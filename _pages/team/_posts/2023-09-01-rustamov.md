Jaloliddin Rustamov is a joint PhD student at United Arab Emirates University and KU Leuven working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical image computing, and neonatal brain imaging. His research focuses on improving neonatal image analysis and structural brain connectivity reconstruction. His current work investigates multi-component diffusion MRI and tractography methods for characterizing white-matter maturation and developing connectivity-based markers of early brain development.
